About qArt-Code 🎨📱

qArt-Code is a fully decentralized, custom QR studio designed for creators in the Artizen and ArtFi ecosystem.

This is where art and utility intersect. We don't make plain, gloomy QR codes. We give them glimmer, shine, and personality—perfect for linking physical art, digital grants, or repository funds on IPFS.

What to do:

  • Paste your project URL or IPFS hash.
  • Upload your own creative logo for the center overlay.
  • Customize colors, shapes, and styles until it pops.
  • Download as PNG for web or SVG for print.
