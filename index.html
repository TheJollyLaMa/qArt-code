About qArt-Code

qArt-Code is a custom QR studio designed for creators in the Artizen ecosystem.

A decent place where art and utility intersect.

What to do:

  • Paste your project URL or IPFS hash.
  • Upload your own creative logo for the center overlay.
  • Customize colors, shapes, and styles until it pops.
  • Download as PNG for web or SVG for print.
